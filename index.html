01

I'm a Senior Motion Designer at Foxymoron, one of India's leading digital agencies. With 6+ years of experience, I bridge traditional post-production with cutting-edge AI video generation.

My work spans branded commercial content for some of India's biggest consumer brands — including Puma, Pond's, Mentos, Onitsuka Tiger, TVS, Converse India, PhonePe, Sandisk, Snitch, and luxury labels like IRTH.

I specialise in translating brand language into precise motion — whether that's a 360° sneaker reveal, a liquid product morph, or a full AI-generated campaign built frame by frame.
